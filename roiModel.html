The AI Advantage

The constraint AI has changed

The pattern is familiar: the caseload is there, but the operation can’t move cases faster without adding staff — and adding staff compresses margin. AI has changed that — platforms built specifically for plaintiff PI practice, now in use by more than 3,000 firms nationally, allow the same staff to handle materially more cases, produce better-documented demands, and settle faster, with documented results showing pre-lit revenue growing by 75% or more and net profit in many cases doubling. The staffing challenge is only getting harder: industry research consistently shows firms expect to lose 20–40% of legal support staff to retirement over the next several years, with an insufficient pipeline of trained replacements.

  • AI prepares demand packages in a fraction of the time — replacing a repetitive, error-prone manual process with one that is consistent and reliable across every case
  • Medical records that arrive incomplete, out of sequence, or with treatment gaps are now detected and addressed proactively by AI — ensuring nothing that affects demand value gets missed
  • AI-assisted preparation produces better-documented demands that drive settlement values 10–20% higher and compress timelines — accelerating cash flow and freeing staff capacity for new cases

A note on trust and data: the platforms integrated into pre-lit workflows are not general purpose AI tools. They are closed-loop systems that work exclusively from the documents in each case file — eliminating the hallucination risk behind the now well-known examples of fictional case citations when lawyers use general purpose tools like ChatGPT for legal work. Client data is not used to train the underlying models and is not shared across firms.

Achieving these results — and maintaining the verification standard that 99% of plaintiff attorneys require — demands an attorney with the operational discipline and experience to select and integrate the right platforms so that workflows perform consistently, and the legal judgment to oversee every output.

The engagement

Of Counsel to the firm — defined scope, results-based compensation

I join personal injury firms as Of Counsel to help identify and deploy the AI platforms best suited to the firm’s pre-lit practice, integrate them into daily workflows, and provide ongoing attorney oversight of outputs — working alongside the managing partner and existing team at whatever level of involvement best fits the firm.

Compensation is structured around documented improvement above the current baseline. Aside from an agreed retainer, the firm bears no incremental cost unless the arrangement is generating measurable results.

Florida Bar rules permit AI platform costs to be recovered as case expenses — provided the charge is matter-specific and the contingency agreement expressly authorizes it. The leading platforms invoice on a per-case basis, satisfying that requirement. For firms that adopt this practice, the net platform cost to the firm is zero.
